## **Tenured AI — Frequently Asked Inquiries**

### **1\. What is Tenured AI?**

**Tenured AI is an AI-native education and workforce intelligence platform designed to help people build, measure, and demonstrate AI competency.** It combines personalized learning, practical application, skill assessment, and workforce-focused insights to help learners become more AI-ready for school, careers, and the workplace.

---

### **2\. Who is Tenured AI for?**

**Tenured AI is designed for anyone who needs to become more capable and confident with AI.** This includes university students preparing for AI-enabled careers, working professionals looking to upskill, educators, career changers, and organizations developing an AI-ready workforce.

---

### **3\. What can I use Tenured AI for?**

You can use Tenured AI to **learn AI concepts, develop practical AI skills, identify competency gaps, practice applying AI to real-world scenarios, and track your development over time.** Your learning experience can be aligned to your goals, career interests, and level of AI experience.

---

### **4\. How is Tenured AI different from an online course or traditional learning platform?**

Traditional learning platforms often focus primarily on **content consumption and course completion**. Tenured AI is designed around **competency and application**—helping you understand what you know, identify where you need to improve, practice applying AI, and demonstrate your capabilities.

> **The goal isn't simply to complete a course. It's to become AI-competent.**

---

### **5\. What does "AI competency" mean?**

**AI competency is the ability to understand, use, evaluate, and apply AI effectively and responsibly.** It goes beyond knowing AI terminology or how to use a particular tool. Tenured AI focuses on developing capabilities that can be applied across technologies, roles, industries, and career paths.

---

### **6\. How does Tenured AI personalize my learning experience?**

Tenured AI uses information about your **goals, experience, progress, demonstrated skills, and areas for improvement** to help guide your learning journey. Rather than assuming every learner needs the same content, the platform is designed to help you focus on the competencies most relevant to you.

---

### **7\. How does Tenured AI measure my AI competency?**

Tenured AI looks beyond simple course completion. **Competency can be evaluated through learning activities, assessments, practical exercises, and demonstrated application of skills.** The goal is to provide a more meaningful picture of your AI capabilities—including strengths and areas where additional development may be valuable.

---

### **8\. Can Tenured AI help me become more prepared for my career?**

**Yes. Workforce readiness is a core part of Tenured AI.** The platform is designed to connect AI competencies with real-world roles, industries, and career pathways so that learners can develop skills that are relevant to the work they want to do.

For university students, Tenured AI can **complement a traditional degree by helping them develop the AI capabilities increasingly relevant to their chosen field.**

---

### **9\. Is Tenured AI only for people with technical or AI backgrounds?**

**No.** Tenured AI is intended for learners across different levels of experience and backgrounds. You don't need to be a programmer, data scientist, or AI expert to get started. Learning pathways can range from foundational AI literacy to more advanced technical and professional competencies.

---

### **10\. How do I get started with Tenured AI?**

**Getting started is designed to be simple.** Begin by creating your Tenured AI account, sharing your learning or career goals, and completing the initial onboarding experience. Tenured AI can then help identify an appropriate starting point and guide you toward relevant learning and competency development.

**Start building your AI competency today.**
