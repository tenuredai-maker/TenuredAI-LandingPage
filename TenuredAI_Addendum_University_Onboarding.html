A2
The Instrument · What the Genesis MOU Actually Says

It is a revenue agreement, not a purchase order.

The Genesis Institution MOU is a perpetual, ledger-anchored revenue agreement. Your institution is a counterparty receiving settlement, not a customer paying a vendor. That single structural fact removes procurement, competitive bid, and capital-approval friction from the entire onboarding path.

THE INSTITUTION COMMITS TO FOUR THINGS
  • · Name one Genesis cohort. One senior cohort in your strongest program — 80–150 students is the recommended pilot band.
  • · Seat a faculty steering committee. Three to five faculty, chaired by a department head, to govern ontology mapping and Hard-Gate alignment.
  • · Open a read-scoped SIS/SSO channel. SAML 2.0 or OIDC, plus an enrollment-verification endpoint. Registrar and IT, roughly 20 engineering hours.
  • · Designate a settlement recipient. One office of record — typically the Treasurer, the Foundation, or the Provost's discretionary fund — to receive quarterly dividend settlement.
TENURED AI COMMITS TO FOUR THINGS
  • · Both settlements. 40% of the per-semester Access Fee (50% if private), and 40% of every transaction originating from your alumni in perpetuity — two separate instruments, both ledger-enforced at the smart-contract layer. Detailed at §A7.
  • · Zero platform cost to the institution — infrastructure, Forge drills, Proving Ground sandboxes, credential issuance, and settlement rails at our expense.
  • · F-100 Dividend Dashboard provisioned to your finance office at settlement go-live, with departmental yield attribution and forensic inflow audit.
  • · Full FERPA / GDPR documentation delivered to your General Counsel before technical intake begins, not after.
Term · perpetual · Termination · 180-day notice · Credentials survive termination

What is not in the MOU: no exclusivity on your students, no restriction on your other partnerships, no curriculum control, no enrollment mandate, no minimum-volume guarantee, and no obligation to continue past the pilot cohort.

Compliance Posture

FERPA by architecture, not by policy

The platform never receives an educational record as FERPA defines it. It receives an enrollment attestation and a consent token. Assessment outcomes belong to your faculty; performance telemetry belongs to the platform; neither party can see the other's domain. Your office sees aggregate cohort trajectory under k-anonymity. It never sees an individual score.

Sandbox Isolation

The Proving Ground never touches your network

Student execution environments run in Zero-Knowledge Session Isolation on platform infrastructure. No agent, container, or process originating in a Forge drill has any route to institutional systems. Your security review will confirm there is no inbound path to defend.

A5
Student Enrollment · The Consent-First Signup Path

Five steps. Individually opted in. The student owns the asset.

No student is enrolled by the institution. Every student opts in personally, through your own identity provider, against a consent surface that itemizes exactly what is shared and with whom. A student who declines remains fully enrolled in their degree program with no academic consequence of any kind.

STEP 01

Institutional SSO

The student clicks an invitation and lands on your own login page. They authenticate with the credentials they already have. Tenured AI never sees a password, never issues one, and never becomes an alternate identity provider for your campus.

STEP 02

The consent surface

A single itemized screen: what is verified, what is published, what stays private, what the institution can see, what a recruiter can see, and what happens on withdrawal. Granular toggles, not a blanket accept. Default state on every optional field is off.

STEP 03

Passport mint

The Sovereign Passport is issued to the student — not to the institution, not to the platform. It carries a k-anonymous public surface showing verified competency bands with zero personal identifiers until the student authorizes a reveal.

STEP 04

Forge drills in-calendar

Drills run inside the academic year at points the faculty steering committee chose. Telemetry accrues to the Passport. The student watches their own AICI, AIOI and AIBS trajectories build across the term in the Proving Ground console.

STEP 05

Hard-Gate & certificate

Senior-year Adversarial Audit Gate clears. A Consensus Certificate mints against the Merkle root and anchors on-chain. The credential is independently verifiable without the platform's servers — and it survives the platform.

STUDENT RIGHTS · NON-NEGOTIABLE
  • · Owns the Passport outright
  • · Controls every reveal and redaction
  • · Pays nothing additional, at any point
  • · Exits with credentials intact via Merkle proof
  • · Declines with zero academic consequence

The 40% institutional dividend is the platform's payment for verification infrastructure. It is never a charge against the student.

A7
The Economics · Two Different 40/40/20s

Same ratio. Different instruments. Do not conflate them.

Onboarding is the decision; the splits are the arithmetic that follows. There are two, and because both divide 40/40/20 they are routinely treated as one thing in briefing. They are not the same money and they do not settle on the same schedule. The Access Split divides a per-student enrollment fee. The Dividend Split divides a per-hire transaction. Your institution receives both, independently.

The distinction in one line

The Access Split divides the $100 Sovereign Access Fee charged per enrolled student, per semester. Termly. Scales with headcount. Settles from the first term of the pilot cohort, whether or not a single graduate has yet been hired.

The Dividend Split divides the transaction value a graduate generates after leaving. Perpetual. Scales with career outcomes. Begins at first Hard-Gate clearance and continues for the length of the alumna's career.

$100 per student · per semester

SETTLEMENT TWO · THE DIVIDEND SPLIT

40% of every alumna transaction

A separate instrument on a separate base — no student fee is involved. Every recruiter reveal, every Performance Bond premium, every Hard-Gate transaction fee, routed atomically, per transaction, forever.

SETTLEMENT PROPERTIES
  • · Atomic. Routes in real time. Never accrues, never escrows.
  • · Cryptographically locked. Enforced at the contract layer. The platform cannot withhold or re-route it.
  • · Auditable. Every routed dollar carries a Merkle proof. Audit to the cent without trusting us.
  • · Perpetual. Attaches to the alumna, not the enrollment year. A 2027 graduate still settles in 2047.
