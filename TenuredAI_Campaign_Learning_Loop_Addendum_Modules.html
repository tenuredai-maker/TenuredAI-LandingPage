CAMPAIGN ADDENDUMLearning Loop · Education Modules track · v1.0

The reading earns TP too.

An addendum to the Learning Loop launch campaign that integrates the Education Modules track — articles, videos, podcasts, annotated source. Where the original campaign sold the drill (Forge / Quiz / Flashcard), this addendum extends the daily-practice narrative to include the content consumption that prepares the drill. The TP economy now spans both surfaces; the campaign must reflect that. This document is additive — it does not replace any asset in the original Learning Loop campaign library; it extends the brief, voice, visual, paid, owned, sales, and measurement coverage to include the M-3xx content surfaces.

§00Brief Delta

What changes in the strategic objective.

The original Learning Loop brief targeted 40,000 Forge sessions and 12,000 active 14-day streaks. This addendum extends the targets to include the content-consumption surfaces while preserving the original drill-focused metrics.

BRIEF · 01 Additive Strategic Targets
  • New primary. 80,000 module sessions completed in 90 days (article + video + podcast + source combined). Module sessions count alongside Forge sessions for streak credit.
  • New secondary. 24,000 comprehension checks passed at ≥ ×1.5 multiplier (Moderate or above confidence with all 3 correct). This is the engagement-quality signal.
  • New tertiary. 18,000 substantive annotations authored across module sessions. Per PAT-020, this is the most durable cross-surface practice signal.
  • Adjacent. 4,200 modules surfaced by the Tenured Agent's decay-driven recommendations and then completed within 7 days. This is the Loop-to-Modules-back-to-Loop pathway in action.
BRIEF · 02 Why the addendum matters strategically
  • Closing the TP-incentive gap. Before this track, engineers who spent time reading and watching earned no TP for it — only the drill earned. That created a perverse incentive that ran counter to the platform's pedagogy.
  • The MOOC-fatigue audience reaches us through content. Engineers exhausted by Coursera and Udemy will come to the platform through a recommended article or video before they ever attempt a drill. The content surfaces are the on-ramp.
  • The streak counter becomes durable. Engineers who travel, who have varying-intensity weeks, who can't commit fifteen minutes of drill every day can still maintain their streak with a content module. The streak no longer requires drill-only commitment, which materially increases retention.
  • The Performance Bond data shape improves. Per PAT-002, telemetry from content engagement contributes to the platform's overall ML model of the user's competency. More signal → better model → more accurate bond pricing.
STRATEGIC RESOLUTION

"The drill is the verification. The reading is the foundation. The TP economy now honors both."

The original Learning Loop campaign was right about the drill's primacy. This addendum corrects an asymmetry: the platform's core thesis — that practice compounds toward verified competency — was visible in the drill surfaces but invisible in the content surfaces. The TP economy this addendum implements makes the platform's pedagogy legible on every surface a candidate touches.

§01Voice Additions

Scholarly-practice register, extended to content.

The Learning Loop's voice rules (compounding, specificity of duration, practice not content, decay is honest, the streak as calendar mark) apply unchanged to the modules track. The addendum below adds three sub-register adjustments specific to content surfaces.

VOICE · 01 Three voice adjustments for content
  • The reading is the soil. When describing the relationship between content and drill, the agricultural metaphor is approved: "The reading is the soil; the drill is the seed; the credential is the harvest." This metaphor appears in the Day-12 welcome email and several content pieces.
  • Active presence, not "attention." The platform measures active presence via PAT-002. It does not measure "attention" in the consumer-AI sense (eye tracking, sentiment analysis). Marketing copy must use "active presence" not "attention" — the term respects what the system actually does.
  • The multiplier is honest. When describing the comprehension check, copy must convey that the check is a verification gate, not a "quiz to test you" — it's the moment when time-attested viewing converts to credentialed competency through the same PAT-019 mechanic the drill surfaces use.
VOICE · 02 Additional Approved Vocabulary ON-BRAND
  • Module. The category word for any content unit (article / video / podcast / source). Capitalized when modifier ("a Module session").
  • Active presence. Two words. Refers to PAT-002 telemetry detection of user engagement during content viewing.
  • Time-attested. Hyphenated. The term for time that has been verified as active by PAT-002 telemetry.
  • Comprehension check. The 3-question verification gate at module end. Lowercase. Specific to modules; the Forge has "Quiz Gauntlet."
  • Substantive annotation. An annotation that meets PAT-020 substantive-detector thresholds — ≥ 80 chars, anchored to a specific position, contains connective phrasing.
VOICE · 03 Additional Forbidden Vocabulary OFF-BRAND
  • "Bingeable." "Watch a series." "Binge-worthy." Entertainment-consumption framing. The Modules are study material, not content to binge.
  • "Course." "Class." "Curriculum." Same constraint as the original Learning Loop voice — the platform does not sell curriculum.
  • "Watch and learn." "Listen and learn." Passive-consumption framing the patent-aligned TP economy explicitly rejects.
  • "Eye-tracked." "Attention-measured." "Engagement-scored." Consumer-AI surveillance framing. The platform measures active presence, not attention.
  • "Speed up the video." "2× playback." "Fast track." Speed-as-virtue framing. The TP economy rewards active engagement, not consumption speed.
VOICE · 04 Additional Canonical Phrases CORE
The reading is the soil.
Used in content that introduces the modules track. The agricultural metaphor frames the platform's pedagogy in non-edtech language — the modules feed the drill, the drill produces the credential.
Active presence, not attention.
Used in privacy / surveillance-adjacent contexts. Distinguishes the platform's telemetry from consumer-AI engagement measurement.
The comprehension is the multiplier.
Used to surface the TP-multiplier mechanic. Frames the comprehension check as the conversion moment from time to credit.
