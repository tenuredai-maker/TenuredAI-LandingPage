§00Campaign Brief

The thesis. The audience. The deliverable.

This asset library exists for one strategic objective: to drive qualified, high-intent professionals — engineers, AI operators, mid-career technologists — into their first Hard-Gate within 30 days of the campaign launch. Every asset below ladders to that goal.

BRIEF · 01 The Strategic Objective
  • Primary metric. 10,000 Hard-Gate attempts in the first 60 days of campaign run.
  • Secondary metric. 2,500 Triple-85 Triple-Threat clearances minted to the Sovereign Ledger.
  • Tertiary metric. 50 enterprise inbound inquiries against the EWARD dashboard surface.
  • North-Star metric. 1,000 candidates with a $150K Performance Bond actively attached to a Sovereign Passport.
BRIEF · 02 The Audience Triangulation
  • Primary. Senior IC engineers (5–12 yrs) who deploy AI in production and feel the credential gap personally. They know LinkedIn lies. They want the truth.
  • Secondary. Engineering managers and Heads of AI who are hiring against a broken signal. They watch take-homes get LLM'd. They want a filter.
  • Adjacent. Career-changers and self-taught engineers whose résumés get skimmed. They want a credential the market can't dismiss.
THE STRATEGIC TENSION

"Your résumé is fiction. Your work is the only evidence left."

Every asset must hold this tension. We are not selling a course, a certificate, or a community. We are selling the only credential that cannot be faked by AI — because the test was specifically engineered against AI assistance, and the record is cryptographically sealed. The campaign's emotional center is the relief of being seen for what you can actually do.

§01Sovereign Authority Voice

The tone every word must hold.

Sovereign Authority is not a marketing voice. It is a posture. It speaks like someone who already operates infrastructure that matters — a central bank's chief economist, a senior policy adviser, a partner at a long-tenured firm — to peers, not prospects.

VOICE · 01 Sovereign Authority — The Five Rules
  • Rule 1 · Authority, never enthusiasm. No exclamation marks. No emojis. No "🚀 We're so excited to announce". The product is consequential; the voice matches.
  • Rule 2 · Specificity, never abstraction. "$11,250 annual premium," "Triple-85 threshold," "180-day strike window." Concrete numbers carry the conviction.
  • Rule 3 · The reader is a peer. Sovereign Authority does not condescend. It assumes the reader already knows LinkedIn lies. It treats sophistication as table stakes.
  • Rule 4 · Decisive sentences. Short. Declarative. The voice ends sentences. It does not trail.
  • Rule 5 · Never hard-sell. The voice describes what is true and invites the reader to act on it. Hard-selling would betray the posture.
VOICE · 02 Approved Vocabulary ON-BRAND
  • Verify. Not "test," not "check." Verify carries finality.
  • Anchor. Cryptographic, immutable, on-chain.
  • Earn. Credentials are earned, never granted.
  • Survive. Hard-Gates are survived; the Proving Ground is endured.
  • Sovereign. Used sparingly — for the Ledger, the Passport, the Identity.
  • Triple-85. Always rendered exactly. Always with the hyphen.
  • Hard-Gate. Capitalized, hyphenated, treated as a proper noun.
  • Proof of Friction. The forensic record. Never "session log."
VOICE · 03 Forbidden Vocabulary OFF-BRAND
  • "Game-changing." A retail word. We do not change games; we end them.
  • "AI-powered." Every product is now AI-powered. We are AI-resistant.
  • "Skill up." "Level up." Gaming vocabulary. We verify, not gamify.
  • "Disrupt." Old startup language. We replace.
  • "Join the revolution." No revolutions. Only standards.
  • "Boost your career." Boosting is for retail SaaS. We don't boost; we underwrite.
  • "Empowering." The reader is already powerful. We attest to it.
  • Emojis in copy. Period.
VOICE · 04 The Three Canonical Taglines CORE
Verify human worth.
The platform-level positioning. Used for hero placements, manifesto contexts, the home page, the deck title slides. Three words. Speaks to the entire thesis. The headline that should remain unmoved across the campaign.
Earn the only credential AI cannot fake.
The Proving Ground product positioning. Used for paid-media headlines, performance-focused channels, A/B test top variants. Holds the strategic tension on the credential gap directly.
