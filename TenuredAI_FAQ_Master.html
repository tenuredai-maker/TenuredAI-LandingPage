No system is perfectly cheat-proof. But the platform is engineered specifically against the cheating vectors that have made traditional credentials forensically empty.

ALTFL six-channel telemetry · PAT-002

The AI Learning Telemetry & Feedback Loop (ALTFL) captures six concurrent channels during Hard-Gate execution:

The capture model is structurally distinct from continuous workplace surveillance. It produces a forensic record of a single bounded assessment session, owned by the candidate. The enterprise EWARD dashboard shows aggregate cohort-level OSD signals, not individual PoF data.

Do credentials expire? What is "decay"?
+

Yes — credentials decay over time. The AI field moves fast (vector DB ecosystems shift, model APIs change, regulatory frameworks evolve), and a credential earned in 2024 cannot honestly represent current competence in 2026 without re-verification. The platform's PAT-003 Skill-Decay model assigns each ontology node its own λ coefficient computed nightly from market velocity, regulatory drift, and the user's Grit Moat.

What this looks like in practice: every node on the user's Passport carries its current St state (Active Sovereign / Warning / Re-Verify), color-banded. The Decay Atlas is the user's personal map of where their knowledge is still current and where it's drifted. Refresh Labs restore decayed nodes without requiring a full Hard-Gate retake.

This is honest by design. The alternative is a credential that says "verified four years ago" without disclosure of whether it's still valid — which is what every existing credential does today.

Decay math · PAT-003

Each ontology node n carries:

  • S₀ⁿ — initial competence score on the node at the time of credential issuance
  • λⁿ — node-specific decay coefficient, computed nightly
  • Stⁿ = S₀ⁿ · e^(−λⁿ·t) where t is time since the credential's anchoring
  • T_sⁿ — node-specific Sovereignty Threshold

State machine:

  • Stⁿ > T_sⁿ → Active Sovereign (credential currently valid on this node)
  • 0.75 · T_sⁿ < Stⁿ ≤ T_sⁿ → Warning (Refresh Lab scheduled before drift)
  • Stⁿ ≤ 0.75 · T_sⁿ → Re-Verify (requires Refresh Lab or Hard-Gate retake to restore)

λ inputs per node: market velocity index (how fast that domain is changing), IAR (Innovation Acceleration Rate of associated tools), regulatory drift (whether the underlying compliance frame shifted), and the user's Grit Moat (G — measured engagement and retention pattern that reduces effective λ).

Refresh Lab mechanics restore decayed nodes incrementally — they are bounded engagements, not full Gate retakes. A user who maintains daily Forge can keep most of their Decay Atlas green indefinitely. A user who steps away for two years and returns will see significant red and is routed to a phased re-verification path.

The Passport surface is explicitly honest about decay on the Owner view; the Recruiter view shows the current St state at credential level (not at node granularity by default; node detail is reveal-gated).
