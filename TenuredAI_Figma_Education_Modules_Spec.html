FIGMA DESIGN OPS SPECEducation Modules · M-301 family · v1.0

The content-consumption surfaces. Now they earn TP too.

A production-grade Figma Design Ops specification covering the content-consumption surfaces missing from the original Learning Loop spec — M-301 Module Hub plus five reader-mode surfaces (article, video, podcast, annotated source) and the M-350 Comprehension Check. Critically, this spec defines the Tenure Points economy for content viewing — how passive engagement earns TP, how comprehension multiplies it, how the patents (PAT-002 telemetry, PAT-019 confidence, PAT-020 annotations) make the credit defensible. The drill surfaces credit verification; these surfaces credit the reading and watching that builds the verification capacity.

§01Overview & Loop Integration

Where these surfaces sit. How they connect to L-100.

The Education Modules are an extension of the Learning Loop — they live under the same L-100 Forge Hub umbrella but operate on a different mechanic. Where L-110 (Quiz) and L-120 (Flashcard) credit verification, the M-3xx surfaces credit engagement with the source material that prepares verification. The Tenured Agent surfaces module recommendations into the L-100 Hub the same way it surfaces drill recommendations.

DESIGN ETHOS — THIS FAMILY

"The reading is the soil. The drill is the seed. The credential is the harvest."

The original Learning Loop spec covered the drill — the seed. It assumed the soil was already there. In practice, engineers who arrive at the platform without the right conceptual foundation cannot drill productively. The M-3xx surfaces are the soil — the reading, watching, listening that prepares the conceptual frame the drill then verifies. The TP economy this spec defines is what makes the soil-tending worth doing. Without TP for content engagement, the platform's incentive structure would reward only the verification step and ignore the preparation. That would be incoherent — the platform's premise is that both are necessary.

§02The TP Economy for Content Viewing

Hybrid model. Patent-aligned. Anti-gaming by architecture.

This is the most consequential section of the spec. The TP economy must reward genuine engagement without creating exploitable loops. The model below threads the three patents — PAT-002 (telemetry attests presence), PAT-019 (confidence multiplies value), PAT-020 (annotation persists usefulness).

Critical observation: the spread from 0 TP (scrubbed, no check) to 31 TP (full engagement) is intentionally wide. The platform's data design rewards the engineer who genuinely engaged with the content and is able to demonstrate it. The OL trigger on Underwritten failure is the same mechanic as the L-110 Quiz Gauntlet — overconfidence at the highest stake produces a Performance Bond review, not just a missed multiplier.

ECONOMY · 03 The streak interaction
Streak counter contribution
A completed module (active viewing + passed comprehension check at any tier) counts the same as a Forge drill session for the daily streak counter. Per PAT-006, the streak multiplier applies to module TP exactly as it applies to drill TP.
Streak example
An engineer at the ×1.50 streak tier (10+ consecutive days) who earns 18 base TP from a video module receives 18 × 1.50 = 27 TP credited to the Ledger. The streak compounding is consistent across drill and content surfaces.
Anti-stuff defense
A user attempting to maintain a streak by completing only 2-minute "modules" with a single comprehension question is bounded by the platform's minimum-engagement floor: a module must have ≥ 8 minutes of attested time AND ≥ 3 comprehension-check questions to qualify for streak credit. Below the floor, the module is logged but the streak does not advance.
PATENT-ALIGNMENT NOTE

"The economy is the architecture. The architecture is the patent."

PAT-002 says: telemetry is a verification signal. PAT-019 says: confidence-weighted scoring multiplies stake-aware engagement. PAT-020 says: annotations persist usefulness. The TP economy for content viewing operationalizes all three. The platform cannot credit passive sitting-through as competency — that would betray PAT-019's core claim. The platform cannot credit only comprehension because that would discount PAT-002's telemetry-as-signal claim. The hybrid is patent-coherent.

§03Design Tokens

Inherits Learning Loop. Three module-specific groups.

The Education Modules inherit the Learning Loop token system (surface colors, confidence scale, decay states, multiplier tiers, typography). The module surfaces add three semantic families: module-type (5 types), presence-state (3 states for active/idle/lost), and TP-source-attribution (3 sources matching the economy above).

§06M-320 · Video Player

Lecture playback. Chapter markers. Attention telemetry. Anti-scrub.

The Video Player is the most consequential anti-gaming surface in the spec. The standard video-player UX includes a scrub bar that allows the user to skip to the end and earn completion credit. The M-320 design does not allow scrubbing past unseen content for TP purposes — chapter markers can be navigated, but the time-attestation engine only credits time the user actually watched.

§07M-330 · Podcast Player

Audio with synchronized transcript. Background-listening allowed but flagged.

The Podcast Player handles audio content. Unlike video, podcasts are often consumed in the background (driving, cooking, walking) — the platform must accommodate this without abandoning the active-presence requirement. The compromise: background listening earns reduced base TP (50% rate) and cannot earn the full comprehension multiplier, but it does count toward streak credit.

COMP · 06 component / background-listening-toggle
Purpose
Explicit opt-in for background-mode listening — the user acknowledges that TP accrual will be reduced and the comprehension multiplier will be capped
Default state
Off · user must explicitly toggle on · the toggle's off state shows "TP accrual: 100% rate" and on shows "TP accrual: 50% rate · comp mult capped at ×1.5"
Why this matters
The platform's data integrity requires that the user cannot retroactively claim background mode after losing focus. Background mode must be explicitly chosen before focus is lost; otherwise focus-loss is treated as presence/lost. This prevents users from gaming the system by turning on background mode after the fact to recover lost TP.

The presence-indicator component is the user-facing surface of this telemetry.

§11Annotation Integration (PAT-020)

Annotations made in any M-3xx surface persist against the underlying ontology node per PAT-020. // Background service running for every M-3xx surface. // Emits presence-state events; consumed by the surface's // PresenceIndicator + TP accrual computation. type PresenceState = 'active' | 'idle' | 'lost'; interface PresenceEvent { ts: number; state: PresenceState; segmentStart?: number; segmentEnd?: number; creditable: boolean; } class PresenceTelemetry { private state: PresenceState = 'idle'; private lastInteraction = Date.now(); private tabFocused = true; private currentSegmentStart = Date.now(); constructor(private onTransition: (ev: PresenceEvent) => void) { window.addEventListener('mousemove', this.touch); window.addEventListener('keydown', this.touch); window.addEventListener('scroll', this.touch); document.addEventListener('visibilitychange', this.onVisChange); window.addEventListener('blur', this.onBlur); window.addEventListener('focus', this.onFocus); this.tick(); } private touch = () => { this.lastInteraction = Date.now(); if (this.state !== 'active' && this.tabFocused) { this.transition('active'); } }; private onVisChange = () => { if (document.hidden) this.transition('lost'); else if (this.tabFocused) this.touch(); }; private onBlur = () => { this.tabFocused = false; this.transition('lost'); }; private onFocus = () => { this.tabFocused = true; this.touch(); }; private tick = () => { const now = Date.now(); const sinceInteraction = (now - this.lastInteraction) / 1000; if (this.state === 'active' && sinceInteraction > 8) this.transition('idle'); if (this.state === 'idle' && sinceInteraction > 30) this.transition('lost'); setTimeout(this.tick, 1000); }; private transition(next: PresenceState) { if (next === this.state) return; const ts = Date.now(); // Segment closure: previous segment is creditable iff state was active. this.onTransition({ ts, state: next, segmentStart: this.currentSegmentStart, segmentEnd: ts, creditable: this.state === 'active', }); this.state = next; this.currentSegmentStart = ts; } } export { PresenceTelemetry, PresenceState, PresenceEvent };

§14Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA. Captions, transcripts, presence announcement, screen-reader equivalence for the anti-scrub bar.

The Education Module surfaces are particularly accessibility-consequential because they include video, audio, and visual telemetry feedback — three categories that historically exclude users with disabilities. The architecture below makes each accessible without compromising the patent's claims.

A11Y · 01 Video captions and transcript (M-320)
  • Captions. All M-320 video content ships with closed captions at launch · WebVTT format · captions are the default-on state, not opt-in.
  • Transcript. The synchronized transcript visible below the video player is the screen-reader-equivalent of the video itself · readable independently of playback · clickable to seek.
  • Audio descriptions. Modules whose content depends on visual material (diagrams, code) ship with an alternate audio-described track · the user can toggle between primary and described tracks via the player controls.
A11Y · 02 Podcast transcript (M-330)
  • Transcript-first. The synchronized transcript is a primary content element, not a secondary affordance · sized at reader/article scale (18/32) · same content the audio delivers.
  • Speaker labels. Every transcript line is labeled with the speaker · screen reader announces speaker change on transition.
  • Transcript-only mode. A toggle allows the user to dismiss the waveform player entirely and consume the podcast as text — TP accrues at the article rate (1 per 5 min) in this mode.
A11Y · 03 Anti-scrub bar — screen-reader equivalence

The video-progress-bar's anti-scrub visualization is a visual surface. Screen-reader users receive the same information via aria semantics:

  • aria-label on progress bar. "Video progress. {N}% watched and creditable. {M}% scrubbed and uncredited."
  • aria-live announcement on segment-credit state change. "Segment from 5:00 to 7:30 marked uncredited."
  • Chapter markers. Each marker has aria-label including its watched-credited state.
A11Y · 04 Presence-indicator announcement

Per the platform's broader accessibility commitment, the presence-indicator state announces to screen readers on transition:

  • active. Polite live region: "Active. TP accrual on."
  • idle. Polite live region: "Idle. TP accrual paused."
  • lost (after 30s). Assertive live region: "Presence not detected. TP accrual paused. Interact to resume."

Special consideration: screen-reader users may have legitimate active-presence patterns the platform's mouse/keystroke detector misses (using screen-reader navigation keys differently from sighted keyboard navigation). The presence service includes a screen-reader-detection branch that treats screen-reader navigation events as active-presence interactions.

A11Y · 05 Reduced-motion handling
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce honored everywhere.
  • Comprehension result-card animations → instant appearance · no slide-in or tick animation.
  • Presence-indicator pulse → static color, no pulse.
  • Presence-toast slide-down → fade-in only.
  • Module cross-fade transitions → reduce to 100ms cross-fade.
CLOSING — THE A11Y COMMITMENT

"Engineers with disabilities should not be excluded from the TP economy."

The TP economy is not optional infrastructure. It is the platform's credentialing currency. An accessibility surface that locks out screen-reader users from earning TP would lock them out from credentialing parity. The above architecture closes that gap. Screen-reader navigation events count as active-presence. Transcripts are first-class content. Captions are default. The anti-scrub bar has full aria semantics. The Education Modules ship to AA at launch, with AAA on every text-content path.

Six surfaces. One TP economy. The soil that feeds the seed.
