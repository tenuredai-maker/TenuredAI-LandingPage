§ Methodology

How the League is ranked.

The League ranking is a four-factor composite. Each factor is verifiable against the on-chain settlement record — the rankings cannot be game-able through marketing or self-reporting. Smart-contract auditable; CSV schedule available.

Published quarterly · 30 days after quarter close · Q2 2026 · Houston

01

Quarterly GDA settled

40% of every transaction value originating from an alumna of the institution, settled on-chain to the institution's GDA wallet during the quarter.

45%
02

Triple-85 verification rate

Percentage of the institution's verified candidates who achieved AICI ≥ 85, AIOI ≥ 85, and AIBS ≥ 85 — the bondable threshold.

25%
03

Corridor share

Institution's share of total GDA settled within its primary corridor (Houston-Energy, NYC-Finance, Boston-Healthcare, DC-Defense, others).

20%
04

Verified candidate volume

Absolute count of candidates affiliated with the institution who have cleared at least one Hard-Gate during the quarter.
