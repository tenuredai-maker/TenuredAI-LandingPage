§ The Thesis

Why this company. Why now.

Four structural pillars defend the investment case. Each is independent of the others. The platform's defensibility compounds because no follower can clear all four simultaneously.

Pillar 01

The verification gap is unfilled.

LLMs invalidated every credential-class signal in 2022. No incumbent — LinkedIn, Workday, Coursera, HackerRank — can pivot to verification without cannibalizing core revenue.

Pillar 02

The category is infrastructure, not SaaS.

Three uncorrelated revenue streams: ledger settlement (Visa-class), reserve float (Berkshire-class), index licensing (S&P-class). Composite supports 25–40× multiples.

Pillar 03

The moat is four-layer structural.

21-patent fortress + Polygon-anchored ledger network + 40/40/20 perpetual institutional contracts + two-sided network effects. Each layer compounds independently.

Pillar 04

The window is briefly open.

~36 months until a strategic incumbent mounts defensive entry. After this window, the IP and institutional lock-ins compound into a non-replicable moat.

§ Common Questions

The five questions every prospect asks.

The full FAQ runs to 56 questions across 8 sections (privacy, IP, pricing, platform risk, integration, etc.). The five most-asked are below; the rest live in the Master FAQ.

Why can't a candidate just paste the Hard-Gate question into ChatGPT?
The Adversary Logic Engine (PAT-001) mutates the candidate's sandbox state in ways known only to the platform's server. An LLM the candidate consults can see the symptoms of the mutation but not the underlying cause — it produces a confident answer to the wrong question. Six channels of behavioral telemetry (PAT-002) catch candidates who try anyway. This is the defining architectural property of the platform.
What happens to my credential if Tenured AI goes out of business?
Every Consensus Certificate is Merkle-anchored to four independent blockchains (Polygon, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism). The verification client is open-source. A learner who cleared a Hard-Gate in 2026 can still prove they cleared it in 2046 — independent of whether Tenured AI as an operating company still exists. This is by deliberate architectural design.
How is the bond's $11,250 premium derived?
π = $5,000 × (λ / CLI) × volatilitycorridor. The $5K base is the actuarial floor; λ is the candidate's individual skill-decay coefficient; CLI is the Continuous Learning Index; the corridor multiplier ranges 1.10 (Houston-Energy) to 1.55 (Boston-Healthcare). The portfolio-weighted Phase 1 average is ~$11,250. Chubb's actuarial team is validating signal quality through the pilot partnership.
How is this different from LinkedIn / Coursera / HackerRank?
Those platforms produce signals — self-reported skills, course-completion certificates, point-in-time test scores — that the LLM era invalidated. The verification gap is structural; no incumbent can pivot because pivoting requires publicly admitting their existing signal is broken. Tenured AI sits in the verification layer between credentials and insurance — a category that did not exist before LLMs made it necessary.
Does the platform sell candidate data?
No. The candidate owns the Sovereign Passport. Recruiters pay for the signal the platform produces (Triple-85 status, cross-surface attribution, OL classification) — not for the candidate's personally identifiable information. The candidate controls reveal.
