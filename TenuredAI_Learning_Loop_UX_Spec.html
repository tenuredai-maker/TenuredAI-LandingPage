LOOP / F1Foundations · The Loop, Not the Course

Static credentials decay. So we built a loop.

Every other EdTech platform sells a course you complete. Tenured AI sells a loop you maintain. The Learning Loop has four surfaces — Forge Drills (declarative recall), Refresh Labs (procedural restoration), the Gate Catalog (verification), and the Ontology Atlas (the map of your knowledge state). Every surface is keyed to the same 4,000-node ontology; every action moves a node's λ; every node's λ feeds your bond.

FORGE · DRILL

Declarative recall

Two-Phase Flashcards (PAT-018) measure not just what you know but how confidently you know it — Confidence-Calibration is the second axis.

REFRESH · LAB

A scoped Hard-Gate that targets one decaying node. Restores λ without the full Proving Ground gauntlet.

GATE · CATALOG

Verification

Curated library of Hard-Gates per persona. Briefings, prerequisites, and the path from "qualified" to "Triple-85."

ATLAS · ONTOLOGY

Your knowledge map

The 4,000-node graph showing your active/warning/critical/locked nodes. PAT-020 annotations follow each node across every surface.

Strategy · the loop is the product

Every existing learning platform optimizes for completion — finish the course, get the certificate, never come back. Tenured AI inverts this: completion isn't the goal because credentials decay. The product is the loop — drill, refresh, gate, repeat — that maintains a Sovereign Passport at value. Designing the four surfaces as one continuous interface (with shared ontology, shared annotations, shared telemetry) rather than four separate "modules" is what makes the maintenance loop feel like one practice instead of four products.

LOOP / F2Foundations · The Math of Obsolescence (λ)

Your knowledge half-life, computed nightly.

PAT-003's core invention: every one of the 4,000 ontology nodes carries its own decay coefficient λ, computed from real-time market signals (documentation change velocity, API deprecation rates, framework adoption curves). Your verified score St on node N decays as St = S0·e−λt. The Learning Loop's entire job is to keep your score above the Sovereign Threshold before it breaches.

DECAY FORMULA · PAT-003
St = S0 · e−λ·t
λ = α·MV + β·IAR + γ·SD − G·σ
σ = AIOI/100 (Efficiency Multiplier)

Market Velocity (MV), Inference Automation Rate (IAR), Skill Drift (SD), Grit Moat (G), Efficiency Multiplier (σ). λ recomputes nightly per node.

3-STATE GATE
▸ ACTIVE SOVEREIGN   St ≥ TS · bond fully valued
▸ DECAY WARNING   TS > St ≥ 0.75·TS · Refresh queued
▸ RE-VERIFY   St < 0.75·TS · credential suspended

Δ₂ from the Performance Delta framework — threshold-relative validity signal. State transitions trigger the Refresh Lab queue.

Designer's Note · render the half-life, don't hide it

The decay coefficient is the platform's most unique idea — and most learning UIs would hide it as scary math. We do the opposite: every drill, lab, and gate surface in the Learning Loop shows the λ value, the current St, and the time-to-breach openly. The Inverse Vault treatment (mono amber on dark) borrows finance-terminal grammar; the engineer learns to read their own knowledge half-life the way a trader reads a P&L. Hiding the math would be the same dishonesty as a credit score hiding which behaviors moved it.

LOOP / F4Foundations · 6-Channel ALTFL Telemetry

Six channels. Millisecond resolution. Forensic by design.

PAT-002's ALTFL captures six concurrent telemetry streams during every drill, lab, and gate — not just outcome, but the full process-level signature of how you thought. This is what distinguishes verified human reasoning from AI-mediated answers; this is what the Sovereign Passport's "Proof of Friction" actually is.

Channel Captures Detects
Keystroke Velocity (KV) Inter-keystroke timing at ms resolution Copy-paste from AI (burst-mode signature)
Inference Latency (IL/TTR) Time from failure injection to first diagnostic command AI-typical sub-baseline response speed
Command Precision (CP) Ratio of investigative-to-remediation commands AI's syntactic perfection vs. human iteration
Error Correction Trajectory (ET) Backtracking, modification, reissuance patterns Absent in AI output — present in human work
Verification Outcome (VO) Binary correctness from Phase-2 of PAT-018 The objective truth-axis
Confidence-Calibration (CCT) Gap between declared confidence and verified outcome Overconfidence — the PAT-019 liability signal
Strategy · forensic capture is the product's spine

Every Learning Loop screen leans on at least one of these six channels. The Two-Phase Flashcard surfaces CCT and VO. The Refresh Lab surfaces IL, CP, ET. The Drill Recap visualizes all six as a Time-Series Signature. This isn't a hidden analytics layer — we show the user their own telemetry openly because the platform's pitch is "we measure how you think, not just what you answered" and that pitch is only credible if the measurement is visible.

LOOP / F5Foundations · Confidence-Calibration

Wrong is one failure. Confidently wrong is the liability.

PAT-018 introduces a Two-Phase Flashcard: Phase 1 you declare your confidence (1-5 swipe gesture); Phase 2 you answer. PAT-019 then classifies each event by the gap between confidence and outcome. A Level-5 confident answer that fails (a "High-Confidence Failure") triggers an asymmetric penalty — the score loss is multiplied by the confidence level. This is what makes the platform's bond defensible: a Sovereign Passport that overconfidently fails is more dangerous than one that admits doubt.

REWARDED · CALIBRATED CORRECT

L5 + correct = +1.0× score (full credit, no multiplier). The platform's gold standard: knew it cold, was right.

ASYMMETRIC PENALTY · HIGH-CONFIDENCE FAILURE

L5 + wrong = −2.5× score penalty + Liability flag. L1 + wrong = −0.4× (the honest "I guessed" answer is barely penalized).

Designer's Note · the gesture is the patent

The Phase-1 confidence declaration must feel as native as swiping to delete an email — that's why we use a swipe gesture rather than a button click. The patent (PAT-018) hinges on the gesture being captured before the answer, so the confidence reading is honest, not retroactive. The UX has to deliver this in a single fluid motion (swipe to declare, tap to answer) — otherwise the data is contaminated by the user adjusting confidence after seeing the question. This is one of those rare UX details where the interaction physics is the IP.

LOOP / F7Foundations · Asymmetric Liability Classification

Three liability tiers. One bond.

PAT-019 classifies every High-Confidence Failure into one of three liability tiers based on confidence level, frequency, and node criticality. Each tier triggers a different downstream consequence — from a passive flag, to score recalibration, to Performance Bond OSD recalculation routed to the institutional underwriter. The Learning Loop surfaces all three openly so the user sees the actual stake of every confident answer.

TIER 1 · ADVISORY

L3-L4 + wrong, isolated event. Logged silently to the calibration profile. No score change, no bond effect.

TIER 2 · CRITICAL

L5 + wrong, OR L4 + wrong on a high-stakes node, OR pattern across 3+ events. Asymmetric −2.5× penalty; calibration warning surfaced.

TIER 3 · BOND-IMPACT

L5 failures on bonded competencies (e.g., AI Governance, Energy Safety). Triggers Performance Bond OSD recalibration via PAT-019's financial bridge. Underwriter notified.

Components

Card surface with node ID + question, 5-step confidence ladder (swipe-driven), gesture hint, after declaration: input field appears (text / multiple-choice / drag-drop per JSON config).

Interactions

Phase 1: swipe right (or arrow keys / number row 1-5 for keyboard) declares confidence — irreversible, captured. Phase 2: answer input appears. Phase 3: instant verdict + calibration gap shown.

Integrity

Confidence is captured before any answer-field affordance appears — the patent's mechanism. Backing out resets the card to a new question; the prior confidence is logged as "abandoned" (not re-used).

Pre-swipe

Verifying

Phase 3 transition: brief mono spinner, then verdict + asymmetric scoring delta surface. ~200ms total.

Network drop mid-drill

Local cache holds the session; on reconnect, the in-flight card resumes from where it was. Streak unaffected by a network hiccup.

Strategy · the gesture is captured before the question is reasoned about

If a user could see the question, think about it, then declare confidence — the CCT signal would be contaminated. PAT-018's claim hinges on the gesture happening before the answer affordance appears. We honor this in the UX by literally not rendering the input until after the swipe. A user who tries to "wait and see" can only tap the confidence ladder — there is nothing else to interact with. This is one of those rare cases where the constraint is the feature.

The Pin Annotation chip is the dignifying gesture: the user writes their own note about why they were wrong, in their own words, and that note resurfaces in every future encounter with this node. The mistake becomes self-authored learning.

Designer's Note · the mock is fairness, not a loophole

The Mock Gate exists to fix one specific fairness problem: an engineer who's never seen a Gate-class scenario doesn't know what they don't know, and a first failure on the graded version is partly an unfairness. The Mock lets them encounter the format once, see their TSS, and decide. We rate-limit (1/week per Gate) and watermark loudly so it never becomes a grinding strategy. The verdict is honest ("would pass" / "would fail") because the only way the mock helps is if it tells the truth.

LOOP / ★Designer's Notes

Eight decisions that make the Loop work as one practice.

The throughlines a future team must preserve to keep the Learning Loop coherent rather than fragmenting into four loosely-related "modules."

01

Four surfaces, one ontology, one ledger.

Forge, Refresh, Gates, Atlas all key to the same 4,000-node graph and write to the same Sovereign Ledger. Designing them as independent modules would shatter the loop.

02

The Loop is a maintenance practice, not a course.

No "complete" state. No finish line. Users are protecting bond value, not collecting badges. Surface copy reinforces maintenance, never completion.

03

Show the math openly.

λ, σ, St, TS, asymmetric penalty multipliers — all surfaced in mono, never hidden. The user learns to read their own half-life the way a trader reads a P&L.

04

The gesture is the patent.

PAT-018's Phase-1 confidence swipe must happen before the answer affordance appears. The constraint is the IP. Don't optimize it away to "speed things up."

05

High-Confidence Failure honored, not punished.

The asymmetric penalty is shown in full mono detail, but the language is composed and the dominant CTA is constructive. A failure becomes a queued lab — never a scolding.

06

Annotations are the user's, not ours.

PAT-020 annotations follow nodes across every surface. Export-as-Markdown is never gated. The user's personal knowledge handbook belongs to them — and that ownership is what makes the platform a credible career-long home.

07

The Mock Gate is fairness, not a loophole.

One mock per Gate per week, watermarked loudly. Existing only to fix the unfairness of being graded on a format you've never seen. Telemetry captured but never routes to bond.

08

The JSON config is the platform's moat.

PAT-021's property-driven drill renders every vertical from one codebase. Medical, legal, financial, K-12 — same component, same telemetry, same ledger. Showing the JSON schema openly (D4) positions Tenured AI as "the OS for credentialing platforms" rather than a closed product. A competitor would need to rebuild not just the rendering layer but the override resolver, the telemetry hooks, the ledger integration — every subsystem the hooks connect to. The configurability isn't a feature; it's how the platform reaches state monopoly velocity without engineering scaling linearly.
