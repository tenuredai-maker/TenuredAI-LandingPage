Tenured AI is the verification infrastructure that restores demonstrable competence to state-funded degrees. Onboarding your state does not require a bill, a budget line, a mandate on any institution, or a vote of any faculty senate. It requires an executive MOU naming one corridor and three flagship institutions.

The institutions run their own technical onboarding independently — the state is not a party to it and carries none of its burden. Your office's obligations are four, and none of them is operational.

Two settlements. Same ratio. Different instruments entirely. Reference only · full model in Addendum 01-A §B5

Both settle 40/40/20, and they are routinely confused in briefing. They are not the same money. The Access Split divides a per-student enrollment fee. The Dividend Split divides a per-hire transaction. The first is termly and scales with your public headcount; the second is perpetual and scales with corridor employment. Your Treasury receives 20% of both, on separate settlement schedules.

SETTLEMENT ONE · THE ACCESS SPLIT

The $100 per-student, per-semester fee

The Sovereign Access Fee is charged per enrolled student, per semester, at participating public institutions. It is the platform's access instrument and the larger near-term line for Treasury, because it settles every term whether or not a single graduate has yet been hired.

SETTLEMENT TWO · THE DIVIDEND SPLIT

20% of every in-state transaction, in perpetuity

A separate instrument on a separate base. Every recruiter reveal and every Performance Bond premium issued against a hire in your corridor routes 40/40/20 at the moment of transaction — attaching to the graduate rather than the enrollment year.
