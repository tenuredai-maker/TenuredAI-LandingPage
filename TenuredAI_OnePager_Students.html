Why bother · the short version
  • Your degree certifies access to AI, not skill with it. Employers have noticed. 75% of HR leaders now weight demonstrated skills over the credential.
  • A Passport is the thing you can hand them instead. Sealed artifacts, adversarially scored, with the work attached — not a line on a resume.
  • It compounds. Four years of telemetry is a Career Memory, not a certificate. It's the part of your record that keeps working after graduation.
Fair questions
  • "Is this more work?" Fifteen minutes a day, scoped to coursework you're already doing. One 90-minute gate a term.
  • "Can I fail publicly?" No. Failed attempts are invisible to everyone including your school. Only cleared Gates mint.
  • "What if I transfer or drop?" The Passport is yours. It moves with you and stays verifiable regardless of enrollment status.
