Two settlements. Same ratio. Different instruments entirely. Reference only · full model in Addendum 03-A §A7

Both settle 40/40/20, and they are routinely confused. They are not the same money. The Access Split divides a per-student enrollment fee. The Dividend Split divides a per-hire transaction. One is termly and scales with headcount; the other is perpetual and scales with career outcomes. Your institution receives both, independently, on separate settlement schedules.

SETTLEMENT ONE · THE ACCESS SPLIT

The $100 per-student, per-semester fee

The Sovereign Access Fee is charged per enrolled student, per semester. It is the platform's access instrument, and it is what the 40/40/20 most often refers to. It settles every term, whether or not a single graduate has been hired yet.

SETTLEMENT TWO · THE DIVIDEND SPLIT

40% of every alumna transaction, in perpetuity

A separate instrument on a separate base. Every recruiter reveal and every Performance Bond premium issued against a hire routes 40/40/20 at the moment of transaction — attaching to the alumna rather than the enrollment year, for the length of her career.

Both splits are enforced at the smart-contract layer and carry a Merkle proof. The platform never holds your share, so it cannot fail to remit it. All figures are modeled projections against stated inputs — not booked revenue, not a guarantee, not a forecast of your institution's outcome.
