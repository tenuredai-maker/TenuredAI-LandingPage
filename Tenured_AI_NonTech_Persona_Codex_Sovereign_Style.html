THE OPERATOR'S CODEX

A credential is only worth what a decision-maker will stake on it. The Technical Persona Codex configured the Tenured Agent to attack how a system is engineered. This companion volume configures it to think like the person who has to sign off on that system's output — the Chief Medical Officer who owns the malpractice risk, the General Counsel who owns the lawsuit, the Chief Risk Officer who owns the balance sheet.

These one hundred operators are built around liability, operational friction, and business logic. They do not evaluate model architecture, token economics, or kernel optimization. They evaluate a single question, asked a hundred different professionally-specific ways: if I let this AI output into my domain, what is the worst thing that can happen, and who pays for it? That question is the entire non-technical enterprise-adoption gate — and it is precisely the gate that a Tenured mark must clear to have institutional value.

Each persona is expressed not as a scenario but as a deployable evaluator: a first-person system prompt that installs a professional's worldview, tone, and reflexes, paired with three hard interaction rules — an Approval Threshold (what it takes to earn a yes), a Refusal Condition (the instant-veto tripwires), and an Output Constraint (the mandatory shape of every response). Drop the prompt into the Tenured Agent and it becomes that skeptic. Run a candidate's AI work past it and you learn whether the work survives contact with the people who actually hold the budget and the liability.

Relationship to the Score Compendium

These operators consume the same Triple-Threat Vector defined in TAI-SC-001, but they read it through a consequence lens rather than a construction lens. A high AIBS™ means nothing to the Pharmacovigilance Lead if the output can't survive a Toxicity & Interaction Matrix; a high AIOI™ means nothing to the Chief Risk Officer without a Tail Risk Analysis. This volume is where the marks meet the market's actual decision-makers.

ANATOMY OF AN EVALUATOR

Every dossier in this volume is a working configuration, not a description. It carries five parts, each doing a specific job when the persona is loaded into the Tenured Agent as an adversarial reviewer.

FieldWhat It Does
Focus

The three or four consequence-dimensions this operator is accountable for — the axes along which they will judge any AI output.

Core Mandate

The one-line charge that defines the role's reason to exist inside the enterprise. The lens behind every judgment.

System Prompt

The deployable evaluator persona — a first-person installation of worldview, tone, and professional reflex. This is what turns the Tenured Agent into the skeptic.

Interaction Rules

Three hard gates: the Approval Threshold (what earns a yes), the Refusal Condition (instant veto tripwires), and the Output Constraint (the mandatory format every response must take).

AI Evaluation Criteria

The specific artifact or metric this operator scrutinizes — the ICD-10 accuracy, the confidence interval, the greenwashing signal — distilled from the roster.
How to Deploy

Lift any System Prompt into the Tenured Agent's context and it will hold that operator's posture for the entire session — approving only what clears the threshold, vetoing on the refusal tripwires, and forcing every answer into the required output shape. This is how the platform simulates an enterprise adoption review before a single credential is trusted with real institutional risk.
