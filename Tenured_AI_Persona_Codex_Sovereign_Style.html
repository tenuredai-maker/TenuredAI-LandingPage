HOW TO READ THE CODEX

A score needs a battlefield. The four marks of the Score Compendium — AICI™, AIOI™, AIOI-ED™, AIBS™ — are meaningless without the adversarial conditions that produce them. The Persona Codex is that condition set: a hundred precise configurations for the Tenured Agent, each one a real profession with its real failure modes weaponized into an evaluation.

Inside The Proving Ground™, the Tenured Agent inverts. On platform routes it is a Mentor, protecting score health and maximizing talent velocity. The moment a persona session begins, it becomes a precise, adversarial system evaluator — scaling its internal execution parameters, injection permissions, and grading weights to the active Persona_ID. The personas are not job titles printed on a badge. They are the machinery that decides what the badge costs.

The hundred are organized into ten Impact Tiers, from the lowest-entropy infrastructure Architects down through the Sovereign Enterprise Orchestrators. Each tier carries a characteristic Primary Focus (the marks its sessions weight most heavily) and a Chaos Level (how hard the Agent attacks). A single professional may hold verifications across many tiers; the tiers describe the work, not a hierarchy of worth.

Provenance

This Codex is the narrative expansion of §9 of the Score Compendium. Every dossier below is drawn from the tiered Detailed Configuration documents and the Tenured Agent Core Context Initialization Guide v2.0, which loads the ten-tier hierarchy into the Agent's long-term context for automated scenario generation. TAI-PC / 02Structure · The Four-Part Dossier

ANATOMY OF A CONFIGURATION

Every entry in the Codex follows the same four-part structure. Read it the way a Proving Ground orchestrator does: the Profile tells the Agent who it is evaluating, the Hard-Gate tells it what to weight, the Proving Ground tells it what world to instantiate, and the Chaos Engine tells it how to attack.

FieldWhat It Encodes
The Profile

Who the professional is and — critically — what they actually struggle with in production. The struggle is the seed of the scenario.

Hard-Gate Focus

The score weighting the session telemetry must satisfy (e.g., AIBS 90% · AIOI 10%). This is the bar; a passing session must clear it in the weighted marks, not in aggregate effort.

The Proving Ground

The broken world the candidate is dropped into — a deadlocked pipeline, a bloated budget, a leaking route. Always a real failure, never a quiz question.

The Chaos Engine Setting

How the Tenured Agent attacks mid-session — the injection, flood, throttle, or spoof calibrated to that profession's specific fragility. The wind against the bar.
Reading the Hard-Gate

A Hard-Gate of "AIBS 90% · AIOI 10%" does not mean the session is 90% coding. It means 90% of the verification weight rests on demonstrated construction mastery and 10% on orchestration judgment. The same broken world, scored under a different gate, certifies a different professional. The gate is the lens; the chaos is the pressure.
