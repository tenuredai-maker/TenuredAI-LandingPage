TAI / 01Frontmatter · Thesis

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — The End of the Single Number

The prevailing paradigm in competency assessment is the single-score model: administer a test, print a number, call it a measure of a human being. That model collapses in the AI-native economy, because market utility now derives from three categorically different cognitive capabilities that are not strongly correlated — theoretical literacy, operational orchestration, and engineering construction.

This Compendium is the canonical register for the platform's answer: the Triple-Threat Scoring Engine, a quantification layer (TAI-PAT-011) that processes forensically verifiable session telemetry through three parallel, independently calibrated heuristic scoring engines, plus a Weighted Multi-Factor (WMF) sub-engine for institutional and educational leadership. The four marks read as a logical hierarchy — Competency (I know it) → Orchestration (I manage it) → Builder (I create it) — with the -ED designation certifying those who govern and disseminate.

The marks are deliberately split between Indices and a Score. An Index is a standing — where you rank in the world. A Score is a performance — how you executed on a specific build. The psychological distinction is intentional: AICI™ and AIOI™ describe your position on the ledger; AIBS™ describes what you shipped under fire.

The second half of this volume — The Persona Codex — is what prevents the marks from decaying into résumé keywords. Each of the 100 personas, organized into 10 technical impact tiers, is a precise adversarial configuration for the Tenured Agent inside The Proving Ground™: a Hard-Gate score weighting, a broken-world scenario, and a Chaos Engine setting calibrated to that profession's real failure modes. A score on this platform is not a self-report. It is the residue of survived friction.

Doctrine · Verification over Decor

Every mark in this Compendium is defined by Functional Competencies that can be independently audited — Blind Prompt Challenges, Workflow Logic Maps, repository scans, forensic telemetry replays. Scores above the 85% threshold trigger a mandatory Proof-of-Work deposit into the Evidence Locker. This is the professional moat: the score is only as strong as the evidence chained beneath it.

2.1 — Why Three Engines, Not One Rubric

A unidimensional score conflates dimensions that the market prices separately. A brilliant prompt strategist may be a mediocre systems builder; an elite RAG engineer may be a hazard in a boardroom. The Triple-Threat Vector preserves the shape of a professional's capability instead of flattening it — which is why the canonical visualization for these marks is a Radar Chart or high-density financial health bar, never a generic progress bar. The shape is the signal.

7.2 — λ-Decay: The Mark That Breathes

Every competency node carried by a mark is subject to the exponential decay model disclosed in PAT-003 — S(N, t) = S₀(N) × e^(−λ_N · t) — where each of the 4,000+ ontology nodes carries its own decay coefficient, cross-referenced against a real-time Market Velocity Index. When decay crosses domain-specific thresholds, the system escalates through a three-tier adaptive response: Notify (soft threshold), Recalibrate (hard threshold — learning path modification and credential expiry warning), and Re-Verify (critical threshold — Chaos Agent dispatch for full node re-verification inside The Proving Ground™, with credential suspension).

Anti-Gaming · Autopilot Detection

The telemetry layer continuously evaluates sessions against a five-condition autopilot criterion — including the confidence-coherence break, where declared confidence holds steady while verified accuracy fluctuates. Genuine expertise is distinguished from autopilot by residual keystroke variance, exploratory command behavior, and calibrated confidence. Upon detection, a Chaos Scenario is dispatched mid-session. You cannot coast to a Tenured mark.

8.2 — How Δ Animates the Marks

Read the four Specimen Plates through the Δ taxonomy and the system snaps into focus. The Type-A deltas (Δ₁, Δ₃) are the growth channel — they decide how quickly an AICI™, AIOI™, or AIBS™ value rises and how aggressively the next Proving Ground session scales. The Type-B deltas (Δ₂, Δ₅) are the validity channel — Δ₂ guards each mark’s credential state while Δ₅ ranks the refresh work that defends it. 8.3 — The Financial Bridge: From Δ to the Bond

The deltas do not stop at pedagogy — they price risk. The PAT-018 two-phase verification substrate captures declared confidence before verified accuracy, and PAT-019’s Asymmetric Liability Classification scores the gap with asymmetric penalties: an overconfident miss is weighted more heavily than an underconfident one, because overconfidence is what institutions actually pay for in production failures.
